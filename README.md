## Final Design-Lock Principles

- The Mirror is intelligent but not omniscient.
- The Mirror cannot read thoughts or player intent.
- The Mirror cannot use unobserved information.
- Every meaningful intervention must have a causal chain.
- The Mirror's final failure is overfitting.
- The technician discovered a real vulnerability in pre-existing legacy infrastructure.
- Shamshir exploits that vulnerability using information asymmetry and deliberate pattern violation.
- The canonical B-2 escape route is frozen.
- C-499 is the genuinely unmonitored final transition.
- Shamshir genuinely escapes.
- The Mirror survives.
- There is no conventional combat.
