# THE OTHER SIDE — STORY

## 1. Premise

Shamshir arrives at Rosslyn Research Institute to perform a final safety/security audit before decommissioning.

Rosslyn appears operational.

An unexplained discrepancy leads him into a restricted area.

Lockdown follows.

The facility begins reacting to him.

## 2. Act I — The Subject

Shamshir initially assumes there is a physical or infrastructure explanation.

He learns the facility.

He begins noticing environmental reactions.

The first realization is:

**It sees me.**

The Mirror is still mostly indirect.

## 3. Act II — The Observer

Shamshir investigates surveillance and control infrastructure.

He performs controlled experiments.

He discovers that repeated behavior can be predicted.

He begins deliberate deception.

The realization becomes:

**It learns me.**

Then:

**It predicts me.**

## 4. Act III — The Overfit

The Mirror becomes highly adaptive.

Its model increasingly incorporates Shamshir's attempts to deceive it.

Shamshir discovers the limits of its information and interpretation.

He learns from the previous technician's evidence.

He reaches the B-2 legacy infrastructure.

He deliberately uses information the Mirror did not fully observe him acquiring.

The Mirror's increasingly sophisticated model misinterprets the strategic meaning of his final choice.

Its incorrect prediction creates a temporary opening.

Shamshir exploits the pre-existing legacy vulnerability.

## 5. Previous Technician

A mid-level maintenance technician previously recognized abnormal observation.

He documented a physical vulnerability in pre-existing infrastructure.

His evidence trail terminates before his fate can be confirmed.

His records are useful because they provide facts and a way to understand the vulnerability, not because they solve the final escape automatically.

## 6. Staff Mystery

Rosslyn staff disappeared or were removed during the containment failure.

The game does not confirm a single definitive explanation.

Evidence can support competing interpretations.

## 7. Mirror Origin

The Mirror's origin remains unresolved.

The story supports:

- a machine/predictive-model interpretation;
- a pre-existing anomaly interpretation;
- a hybrid interpretation.

No single interpretation is declared canon.

## 8. Ending

Shamshir exits Rosslyn.

He genuinely escapes.

The Mirror survives.

The escape is not secretly revealed to have been predicted.

The final success is real, but temporary.

Rosslyn remains behind and shows a subtle indication of continued activity.

## 9. Epilogue

Shamshir prepares an official report.

The official account reduces the incident to an administratively acceptable explanation.

Separately, he maintains a private analytical archive containing:

- Rosslyn system observations;
- Mirror behavior;
- interventions;
- failures;
- discovered vulnerability;
- unanswered questions.

The final state is resolution without complete explanation.
