# THE OTHER SIDE — MIRROR DESIGN

## 1. Nature

The Mirror is an intelligent adaptive observational entity/system.

Its fundamental nature remains unresolved.

Machine, anomaly and hybrid interpretations remain possible.

## 2. Information Boundary

The Mirror may use:

- cameras;
- microphones;
- sensors;
- direct observation;
- physical system activity;
- door events;
- terminal events;
- electrical events;
- access events;
- legitimate environmental traces.

It cannot:

- read thoughts;
- directly read intent;
- access arbitrary engine state;
- know unobserved information;
- magically see through architecture;
- retain failed-timeline knowledge after reload.

The Mirror can observe physical reality without necessarily understanding the strategic meaning of what it observes.

## 3. Learning Stages

1. Observation
2. Recognition
3. Prediction
4. Adaptation
5. Deception recognition
6. Overfitting
7. Escape vulnerability

These are conceptual stages, not UI.

## 4. Learning Quality

Learning depends on:

- observation quality;
- observation quantity;
- behavioral consistency;
- contextual relevance;
- complexity.

The Mirror learns imperfectly.

## 5. Deception

The Mirror can eventually identify deliberate false patterns.

It should respond by testing rather than instantly knowing the truth.

## 6. Counter-Test Rule

Every important Mirror intervention must be explainable:

**Observed data → learned pattern → prediction → intervention**

If that chain cannot be reconstructed from the designed world, the behavior is invalid.

## 7. Overfitting

Overfitting occurs when the Mirror assigns excessive significance to a complex behavioral interpretation.

It still sees physical evidence when a legitimate observation pathway provides it.

Its mistake is what the evidence means and how that meaning is incorporated into its behavioral model.

## 8. Final Failure

The final failure is not random stupidity.

The Mirror has progressively learned that Shamshir tests its predictions and deliberately creates false patterns. As a result, it develops an increasingly sophisticated expectation of his late-game behavior.

Shamshir then acquires information through a genuine observation boundary and deliberately uses a legacy infrastructure vulnerability whose strategic significance is not correctly represented in the Mirror's model.

The Mirror sees relevant actions where its observation pathways permit.

It nevertheless interprets the action incorrectly.

Its incorrect prediction produces a causal, temporary opening through already-established facility/system relationships.

The exact physical manifestation of that opening is implementation-level.

The design-level rule is fixed:

**overfit → incorrect prediction → temporary opening → legacy vulnerability exploited → escape**

The Mirror survives.

## 9. Technician Relationship

The previous technician discovered a physical vulnerability in pre-existing infrastructure.

The vulnerability is not supernatural and does not depend on Shamshir.

The technician's evidence allows Shamshir to understand a discrepancy between physical infrastructure and the Mirror's reliable model.

The exact hardware and interaction are not specified at this level.

## 10. Persistence

Save/load restores the Mirror exactly.

A failed timeline cannot teach the next loaded timeline.

## 11. Presentation

The Mirror is communicated through:

- environmental reactions;
- system behavior;
- indirect evidence;
- progressively stronger physical presence.

It does not require a conventional monster identity.

## 12. Validation

No Mirror behavior is valid merely because it is frightening.

It must be:

- causally motivated;
- physically possible within the designed world;
- supported by an observation path;
- consistent with its current learned model;
- bounded by established facility systems;
- incapable of using player intent or hidden engine state.

Exact algorithmic implementation is not part of the Design Lock.
