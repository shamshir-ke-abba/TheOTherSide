# THE OTHER SIDE — HORROR SYSTEMS

## 1. Horror Principle

The horror comes from being understood by something that can use that understanding against you.

## 2. Psychological Horror

The player gradually loses confidence in the assumption that familiar behavior is safe.

## 3. Environmental Horror

Rosslyn communicates threat through:
- changing access;
- altered lighting;
- unusual system states;
- environmental traces;
- infrastructure behavior;
- distant physical presence.

## 4. Systemic Horror

A familiar system becoming strategically hostile is more important than visual spectacle.

Examples:
- a familiar route becomes monitored;
- a door responds differently;
- an expected light state changes;
- a system behaves correctly but at the wrong strategic moment.

## 5. Mirror Presence

Early:
- indirect environmental reaction.

Middle:
- ambiguous physical presence.

Late:
- undeniable obstruction or presence without fully classifying its anatomy.

## 6. Jumpscares

Rare.

They must not replace investigation or prediction mechanics.

The final horror payoff comes from the Mirror being highly intelligent yet still capable of a causal interpretive error through overfitting.

## 7. No Horror HUD

Do not use:
- proximity meters;
- sanity bars;
- conventional enemy HUDs;
- explicit Mirror confidence meters.

The player should infer danger.

## 8. Safety of Interpretation

The game may create uncertainty about meaning.

It should not create uncertainty by breaking its own physical rules.

The player should be able to reason backward from consequences.
