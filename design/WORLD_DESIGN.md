# THE OTHER SIDE — WORLD DESIGN

## 1. Rosslyn

Rosslyn is a believable behavioral research facility in the Pacific Northwest.

It is isolated by dense coastal forest and a controlled perimeter.

The facility appears institutional and operational rather than fantastical.

## 2. Spatial Philosophy

Rosslyn is a dense, interconnected facility.

The player should build a mental map.

The same spaces are revisited under different system conditions.

## 3. Systems Must Be Visible

Important infrastructure should exist physically:
- relay cabinets;
- electrical rooms;
- access panels;
- surveillance equipment;
- mechanical junctions;
- maintenance shafts;
- service documentation;
- architectural plans.

## 4. Information Blind Spots

Rosslyn contains real blind spots:
- air-gapped equipment;
- disconnected hardware;
- old mechanical systems;
- offline documentation;
- physically isolated maintenance routes.

These are necessary for the final information asymmetry.

No unsupported historical date or numerical percentage is required to explain the layered infrastructure.

## 5. Staff Presence

Rosslyn initially appears operational.

After lockdown there is no persistent companion.

Human presence continues through:
- recordings;
- communications;
- documentation;
- environmental traces.

## 6. Contradictory Evidence

The world may contradict itself through:
- official records versus physical evidence;
- routine maintenance versus containment records;
- ordinary research versus unusual protocols;
- newer systems versus older infrastructure.

The contradiction is intentional.

## 7. Exterior

The exterior establishes isolation.

Most gameplay occurs indoors.

The final escape crosses the facility boundary.

## 8. Environmental Storytelling

Narrative information should be distributed through physical space.

The player should infer what happened rather than receive a complete exposition dump.

## 9. World Rule

A major space that has no believable function, system relationship, evidence value, Mirror relevance, or Act-state purpose should be removed or reconsidered.
